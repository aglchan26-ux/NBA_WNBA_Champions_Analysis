Championships: NBA and WNBA eras

Takeaways
  • NBA playoffs punish threes and rim attempts. Points per three-point attempt fell in the playoffs in all 23 seasons (−0.040 points per shot). Rim attempts fell in 87% of seasons. Mid-range shots held up best.
  • WNBA playoffs show no reliable shot-zone penalty. Every zone’s 95% interval includes zero; with 8-12 playoff teams a year the sample is small.
  • The WNBA is about six years behind the NBA on the three-point shift, and the gap has grown. In 2026, 37% of WNBA shots were threes, about where the NBA was in 2019-20. In 2017-2020 the gap was about three years.
  • Recent WNBA champions are ahead of their own league. From 2022 to 2026 they took 5 percentage points more of their shots from three, and 7.5 points fewer at the rim, than the league average. NBA champions have stayed within 3 points of their league.

Everything on this page is association, not causation, and each league is measured against itself.

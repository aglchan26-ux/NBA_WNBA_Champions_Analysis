Who counts

A player-season is counted if the player appeared in at least 15% of team games, or averaged 20+ minutes over at least 6 games (NBA) or 4 games (WNBA), which covers injury-shortened seasons. Uncounted rows stay in the data but do not shape the archetypes, labels or value rankings. Players who were hurt for the playoffs are not counted on their team’s playoff roster.
